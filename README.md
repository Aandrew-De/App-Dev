 # Seven

**Seven** is my favorite psychological crime thriller. Directed by *David Fincher*, it follows two detectives tracking a serial killer who uses the seven deadly sins as his motive.

## Why I Love This Movie

Here are a few reasons why this film is a masterpiece:
*   *Gritty Atmosphere:* The constant rain and dark cinematography create a perfect mood.
*   *Incredible Twist:* The ending is one of the most memorable in cinema history.
*   *Stellar Acting:* The performances elevate the intense script.

### Favorite Characters 

1. Detective William Somerset
2. Detective David Mills
3. John Doe
